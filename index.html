01 / about

I make things precisely
and then let them misbehave.

I work in the space where equations turn into images and images turn into questions. Half of me runs experiments; the other half paints over the results. The pattern behind this page is a de Jong attractor — four numbers, two lines of math, and something that looks like ink.
